Hi! I'm Ravi Sanker S. I completed my undergraduate studies in Computer Science from BITS Pilani and I am currently working as a Software Engineer at DevRev.

I am generally interested in solving challenging engineering problems and love to understand how things work under the hood. One of the goals I'm currently pursuing is to understand everything from above the abstraction layer of logic gates that goes into the making of a basic computer that can compile and execute a C program. After I build a mental model that I'm satisfied with, my next goal is to actually build this basic computer ([inspired by Ben Eater](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLowKtXNTBypFbtuVMUVXNR0z1mu7dp7eH)). 

I started this blog to force myself to pen down concepts that I am interested in. You can find more about my motivations [here](/blog/why).

When I'm not spending time on computers, you will mostly find me running or lifting weights. I also absolutely adore the mountains.
